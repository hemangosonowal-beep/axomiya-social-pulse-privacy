Axomiya Social Pulse

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 October 2026

Axomiya Social Pulse is an Assam-focused research and monitoring tool that identifies and ranks publicly available news, creator, public-service and entertainment videos. This policy explains how the service handles data obtained from Meta platforms.

Data we process

The service may process the following public Facebook Page information:

  • Page names, public identifiers, categories and public URLs;
  • public post captions, publication times, permalinks and video or attachment identifiers;
  • public thumbnails; and
  • aggregate reactions, comments, shares and view counts when Meta makes those metrics available.

The service does not use Facebook Login for members of the public and does not collect private messages, passwords, payment information, private profiles or Instagram account data.

How we use the data

Public Page data is used to discover Assam-related videos, calculate trend rankings, compare engagement over time, prevent duplicate collection, troubleshoot collection failures and maintain the service. We do not use this data to make decisions about individuals.

Retention

Facebook video posts and metric snapshots are retained for up to 30 days in the active collection system, after which the collector removes expired records. Basic watchlist configuration and operational records may be kept longer when needed to operate, secure or audit the service.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell Meta Platform Data. Data may be processed by infrastructure providers used to host the application and its database, subject to appropriate access controls, or disclosed when required by law.

Security

Access tokens and database credentials are kept server-side and are not committed to the public source repository. Stored data is protected using access controls appropriate to the hosting and database services in use.

Requests and deletion

Axomiya Social Pulse does not create public-user accounts. To request removal of a public Page or post from the monitoring dataset, open a request in the project's GitHub issue tracker and include the relevant Page or post URL. Valid requests will be reviewed and the associated stored records removed when appropriate.

Children

The service is not directed to children and does not intentionally collect private information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the service or its data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when a material update is made.

Contact

Privacy questions can be submitted through the Axomiya Social Pulse issue tracker.
